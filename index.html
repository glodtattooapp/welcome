What it does

Built around how a booking day actually goes.

One form your clients fill out, one place where every request lives after that. No inbox digging, no spreadsheet, no copying details by hand.

Your form

Styled to match you, not a template

Pick your own colours, fonts and layout, then choose exactly which questions clients answer. Two artists on Glød can end up with forms that look nothing alike.

Your list

Every request, tracked

New, contacted, booked, declined. Search by name, filter by city, add notes as things move along.

Your numbers

Know what you made, privately

Add a confirmed price to any booking and see it totalled up by month, the full amount, VAT included. Lock the whole tab behind your own PIN, so it's just for you, not whoever's helping out that day. Set your income tax and VAT rates once and it works out roughly what to put aside as you go.

Your invoices

A proper invoice, one click away

Add VAT per booking and Glød works out what to actually charge, before you even send the quote. Invoices number themselves in order, and the description is yours to edit before it goes out. Print it or save straight to PDF.

Your phone

Lives on your home screen

Add it once and it opens like any other app, full screen, no browser bar, no app store required.

Your calendar

Right alongside your list

Connect a Google Calendar and see your actual schedule sitting next to your incoming requests.

Your data

Only ever yours

Signed in as you, seeing only your requests. If another artist joins later, your data stays fully separate from theirs.

Your account

Everything in one settings screen

Set your business name, change your password, and set or change your accounting PIN, all from one tab. Password and PIN changes always confirm the old one first.

Your cities

One form, every city you visit

List every city you take bookings in, home base or guest spot, and clients just pick from a dropdown. One shared form instead of a different one for each stop.

Setup

Getting it onto your home screen

Takes about a minute, and only needs doing once. From then on it opens like any other app.

01

Open your link in Safari

Once you're approved, you'll get a link to your own tracker. Open it in Safari on your phone, not Chrome or another browser, this step only works in Safari on iOS.

02

Tap the share icon

The square with an arrow pointing up, usually at the bottom of the screen.

03

Add to home screen

Scroll down in the share menu, tap "Add to Home Screen," then confirm. Its own icon will appear right there with your other apps.
